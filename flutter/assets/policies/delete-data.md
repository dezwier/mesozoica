# Request data deletion

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This page explains how to remove selected Mesozoica progress while keeping your account so you can continue using the app.

## About Mesozoica

Mesozoica is a location-based paleontology game. For support requests, contact contact@mesozoica.app.

## How to request data deletion

In the Mesozoica app:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Open the **Account** tab.
4. Tap **Delete data**.
5. Choose the categories to remove (site progress, fossil progress, dinosaur progress, and/or skill XP).
6. Confirm **Delete data**.

After confirmation, the selected content is removed immediately and cannot be undone. You must be signed in to complete this request.

## What we delete

When deleting data but keeping your account, Mesozoica removes the categories you select:

- site progress linked to your account;
- fossil progress linked to your account;
- dinosaur progress linked to your account;
- skill XP and career level, if you choose that option.

## What we keep

To let you sign in again, Mesozoica keeps your account identity:

- email and username;
- authentication method (password, Google, and/or Apple links);
- remaining gameplay categories you did not select;
- minimum account metadata needed for access and security.

## Other option

If you want complete removal of both account and data, use **Delete account** instead. See https://learnfromdata.ai/mesozoica/delete-account.

## Contact

For data deletion questions: contact@mesozoica.app.
