# Request account deletion

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Mesozoica account and the data associated with it.

## About Mesozoica

Mesozoica is a location-based paleontology game. For support requests, contact contact@mesozoica.app.

## How to request account deletion

In the Mesozoica app:

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Open the **Account** tab.
4. Tap **Delete account**.
5. Confirm **Delete**.

After confirmation, deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone. You must be signed in to complete this request.

## What we delete

When deleting your account, Mesozoica removes:

- Your account identity (email, username, profile fields, linked sign-in methods, Firebase identity).
- Your gameplay progress (sites, fossils, dinosaurs, tools, skills/XP, walking distance, and related records).
- Your profile media, relationships, notifications, and account-linked device tokens.

## What may remain

In limited cases, some shared or system-level records may be retained when required for:

- legal compliance;
- abuse prevention and platform safety;
- short-lived backup recovery windows;
- integrity of shared resources that other users still rely on.

## Need to keep your account?

If you want to remove selected progress but continue using Mesozoica, use **Delete data** in the same settings area. See https://learnfromdata.ai/mesozoica/delete-data.

## Contact

For account deletion questions: contact@mesozoica.app.
